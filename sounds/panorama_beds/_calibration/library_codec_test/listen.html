How to tell codec from model

  • If WAV sounds scratchy too: the model is the source of the noise. MP3 is not the culprit. No change to delivery format needed.
  • If WAV is clean and MP3 192 is scratchy: compression is adding audible grain on sparse/quiet content. We'd need to consider lossless delivery for library-archetype scenes specifically, OR accept the grain as an acceptable tradeoff for size.
  • If MP3 192 is clean and MP3 128 is scratchy: 192 is fine; 128 is the floor.
